# UTAR Admission Information

Source: UTAR's official admission website (https://admission.utar.edu.my), checked 2 November 2026.
UTAR = Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman. Admissions are handled by the Division of Admissions and Credit Evaluation (DACE).
DACE in Chinese: 招生与学分评估部 (DACE). In Malay: Bahagian Kemasukan dan Penilaian Kredit (DACE).

## Entry qualifications
- Foundation programmes accept: SPM, O-Level / IGCSE, Unified Examination Certificate (UEC), and other equivalent qualifications recognised by the Malaysian Government.
- Bachelor's Degree programmes accept: STPM, UEC, A-Level, South Australian Matriculation (SAM), Canadian Pre-University (CPU), Foundation / Matriculation, Diploma, and other equivalent qualifications recognised by the Malaysian Government.
- Minimum grades differ for each programme. Programme lists: https://study.utar.edu.my/foundation.php and https://study.utar.edu.my/undergraduate.php
- Other qualifications, for example a diploma from a college or polytechnic, are considered case by case.
- Some programmes have additional requirements, for example Medicine (MBBS), Nursing, and Chinese-language programmes (Applied Chinese Language, Chinese Studies, Journalism in Chinese Media).
- Credit transfer: include certified copies of academic transcripts, programme structure and course syllabus with the application.
- Official page: https://admission.utar.edu.my/Entry-Qualifications-and-English-Language-Requirements.php

## English-language requirement
- Most programmes require a credit or a relevant score in English.
- Foundation: applicants below the requirement may get a conditional offer and must join the English Enhancement Programme (EEP) and pass EEP Level 7 within the first two trimesters. Applicants whose previous qualification was taught fully in English are exempted, but still need at least a pass in SPM English or equivalent.
- Bachelor's Degree (Malaysian students): applicants below the requirement who have at least a pass in SPM English may be admitted, but must take the Intensive English Programme (IEP) and pass IEP CEFR B2 before starting the degree.
- International students need a minimum score in a recognised English test such as MUET, IELTS or TOEFL. The exact scores are in the official table on the page above.

## How to apply (6 steps)
1. Register an account and complete the online application form (https://admission.utar.edu.my/Apply_Now.php).
2. Provide the information and supporting documents listed in UTAR's Application Checklist.
3. The outcome is usually released within 7 working days by email and SMS / WhatsApp. It may take longer if credit evaluation, an assessment or an interview is needed.
4. Accept the offer: read the offer letter, then pay the fees shown in the Student Bill by the due date and keep the payment receipt. Acceptance is effective only after payment.
5. The Student ID is created after payment (about 3 working days via JomPAY or Public Bank). Then log in to the UTAR Portal, create the UTAR email account and complete the Student Verification Exercise.
6. Attend the freshmen orientation.
- International students should apply at least 12 weeks before the programme starts. Applications sent less than 4 weeks before may be moved to the next intake.
- Official page for steps 4 to 6: https://admission.utar.edu.my/Acceptance-and-Registration.php

## Documents
- When applying: the supporting documents listed in UTAR's Application Checklist.
- At registration / orientation: Health Examination Report; original and photocopy of educational certificates; MyKad (Malaysians) or passport (international students). MBBS students also submit a Declaration by MBBS Students.

## Intakes (programme start dates)
- Foundation: 25 January 2027 (January 2027 intake), 9 June 2027 (June 2027 intake).
- Bachelor's Degree (except MBBS and Nursing): 25 January 2027 and 9 June 2027.
- Nursing: 9 June 2027 (June 2027 intake). Nursing also has an October intake.
- Medicine (MBBS): one intake per year, in October. The next date has not been announced yet.
- Application closing dates are not published for most programmes.
- Official calendar: https://admission.utar.edu.my/intake-and-Academic-Calendar.php

## Campuses
- UTAR has two campuses: Kampar and Sungai Long.
