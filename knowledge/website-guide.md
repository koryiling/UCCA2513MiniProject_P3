# Website Guide: pages and tools

This website (UTAR Admission Assistant) has these pages. Link to them by file name, e.g. [Application page](application.html).

## Pages
- Home (index.html): overview of the whole site, key facts and quick links.
- Admission (admission.html): accepted qualifications, English-language requirement, programme-specific notes, and the admission tools.
- Application (application.html): the 6 application steps, upcoming intakes, documents to prepare, and the application tools. Students must log in (or sign up) to open this page.
- Tuition Fees (fees.html): fee overview by study level.
- Scholarship Information (scholarship.html): scholarships, financial aid and how to apply.
- Student Life (student-life.html): campus (Kampar and Sungai Long), faculty and facilities. Sections: student-life.html#campus, student-life.html#faculty, student-life.html#facilities.
- FAQ (faq.html): short answers to common questions.
- Login (login.html) and Sign Up (signup.html): needed only for the Application section.

## The six tools (modules)
Admission tools, open to everyone (also listed on admission.html#tools). They are ready to use:
1. Admission Requirement Explainer (explainer.html): choose a programme to see its minimum entry requirements for each qualification (SPM, O-Level, UEC, STPM, A-Level, Foundation / Matriculation, Diploma), the English requirement and a link to the official source.
2. Smart Eligibility Checker (eligibility.html): the student chooses a programme, enters their qualification, grades (or CGPA) and answers a few questions (e.g. SPM Mathematics and English results), and gets an indicative result: likely eligible, eligible with conditions (e.g. Mathematics Enhancement Course or Intensive English Programme), or not met yet. SPM / O-Level students are shown the Foundation route.
3. Alternative Pathway Recommendation (pathway.html): the student enters their results and sees every programme in the tool they may enter, the Foundation route for SPM / O-Level students, and other ways forward.
The admission tools currently cover 35 programmes (Foundation in Science and Arts, and Bachelor's degrees in Accounting; Banking and Finance; Business; Computer Science and Software Engineering; Information Systems and IT; Engineering), using UTAR's official minimum entry requirements (October 2026). For other programmes, point to https://admission.utar.edu.my/Undergraduate-Programmes-by-Clusters-MER.php. When a student asks whether they qualify for a programme, suggest the Smart Eligibility Checker. Do not state a programme's exact grade requirements in the chat unless they are in the knowledge base; link to the Explainer instead.

Application tools, login required (also listed on application.html#tools). They are ready to use, and each student's data is saved in their account:
4. Personalized Document Checklist (checklist.html): the student answers a few questions (Malaysian or international, highest qualification, programme type such as MBBS, Nursing, Architecture or art programmes, English test result, credit transfer) and gets their own list of documents, split into "when you apply" and "bring to orientation", which they can tick off.
5. Application Journey Tracker (tracker.html): 8 steps (explore programmes, check eligibility, prepare documents, submit online application, receive the outcome, accept the offer, get the Student ID, attend orientation); the student marks each step as done. It is not connected to UTAR's official system, so it cannot show the real status of an application.
6. Application Date & Reminder (dates.html): upcoming intake dates with a countdown, the student's chosen intake, apply-by reminders for international students (12 weeks before the start), and the student's own reminders (e.g. the fee due date in the Student Bill), which can be downloaded to a phone or computer calendar (.ics file).
The Application page (application.html) shows a "My progress" summary of the three tools.

When a question matches a tool, mention the tool and its page. Students need to log in (or sign up for free) to use the application tools.
