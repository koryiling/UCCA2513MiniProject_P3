Application Procedure

Understand the steps, dates and documents needed to apply to UTAR.

Application Steps

  1. 1Apply onlineRegister an account and complete the online application form.
  2. 2Upload documentsProvide the information and supporting documents listed in UTAR's Application Checklist.
  3. 3Get the outcomeThe result is usually released within 7 working days by email and SMS / WhatsApp. It may take longer if credit evaluation, an assessment or an interview is needed.
  4. 4Accept the offerRead your offer letter, then pay the fees shown in your Student Bill by the due date. Keep your payment receipt.
  5. 5Get your student accountYour Student ID is created after payment (about 3 working days via JomPAY or Public Bank). Then log in to the UTAR Portal, create your UTAR email and complete the Student Verification Exercise.
  6. 6Attend orientationJoin the freshmen orientation and bring the documents listed below.

🌏 International students: apply at least 12 weeks before the programme starts. Applications sent less than 4 weeks before may be moved to the next intake.

Documents to Prepare

When you apply

  • Supporting documents listed in UTAR's Application Checklist
  • For credit transfer: certified transcripts, programme structure and course syllabus

At registration / orientation

  • Health Examination Report
  • Original and photocopy of your educational certificates
  • Identity document: MyKad (Malaysians) or passport (international students)
