Minimum grades differ for each programme. Check the programme lists for Foundation ↗ and Undergraduate ↗ programmes.

English Language Requirement

Most programmes require a credit or a relevant score in English. If your score is lower, you may still be admitted with conditions:

Foundation

You may receive a conditional offer and must join the English Enhancement Programme (EEP), passing EEP Level 7 within your first two trimesters.

If your previous qualification was taught fully in English, you are exempted, but you still need at least a pass in SPM English or equivalent.

Bachelor's Degree (Malaysian students)

If you have at least a pass in SPM English, you may be admitted but must take the Intensive English Programme (IEP) and pass IEP CEFR B2 before starting your degree.

International students need a minimum score in a recognised test such as MUET, IELTS or TOEFL. Check the official table for the exact scores.

Good to Know

Programme-specific requirements

Some programmes, such as Medicine (MBBS), Nursing and Chinese-language programmes, have additional requirements.

Other qualifications

Other qualifications, for example a diploma from a college or polytechnic, are considered case by case.

Credit transfer

To apply for credit transfer, include certified copies of your academic transcripts, programme structure and course syllabus.
